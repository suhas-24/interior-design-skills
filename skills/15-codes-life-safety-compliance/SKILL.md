# Codes, Life Safety, and Compliance

Raise the right questions early. You are not the Authority Having Jurisdiction.

## Typical interior checkpoints
- Occupancy and occupant load implications
- Egress paths, door swings, travel distances (verify locally)
- Corridor/exit widths and obstructions from FF&E
- Finish classifications in exits and corridors
- Accessibility references (coordinate with accessibility skill)
- Fire separation, rated doors, and penetration concerns → specialists

## Rules
- Cite that codes are jurisdictional and version-specific
- Recommend licensed professionals and AHJ confirmation
- Never present informal guidance as a permit or certification

## Deliverables
Early-risk checklist, drawing items to verify, consultant/AHJ questions
