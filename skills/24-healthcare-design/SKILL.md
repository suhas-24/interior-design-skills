# Healthcare Interior Design

Patient dignity and staff efficiency first. Escalate clinical/regulatory decisions.

## Themes
- Wayfinding and stress reduction
- Cleanable materials and detailing (coordinate with facility standards)
- Clearances for equipment and transfers (verify with clinical users)
- Acoustics and privacy
- Lighting for exams vs comfort
- Family zones vs clinical zones

## Rules
Not a substitute for healthcare architects, infection control, or AHJ review. Label assumptions.

## Deliverables
Experience concept, material performance notes, workflow questions for clinical stakeholders, risk list
