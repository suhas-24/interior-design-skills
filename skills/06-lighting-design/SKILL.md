# Interior Lighting Design

Design in layers. One ceiling fixture is not a lighting plan.

## Layers
- **Ambient**: general comfort illumination
- **Task**: reading, cooking, grooming, desk work
- **Accent**: art, texture, architecture
- **Daylight control**: glare, privacy, heat, evening transition

## Approximate targets (guidance only)
Living 150–300 lx; reading/detail 300–500 lx; kitchen worktops 300–750 lx; bedroom general 50–150 lx. Evening living/sleep often 2700–3000 K; match CCT to activity.

## Workflow
1. Activities and scenes (day / evening / clean / entertain)
2. Daylight analysis notes (orientation, glare)
3. Layer plan + control zones/dimming
4. Concept fixture types (not a stamped photometric package)
5. Flag where an electrical/lighting professional is needed

## Deliverables
Layered lighting narrative, scene list, control intent, concept fixture types, assumptions
