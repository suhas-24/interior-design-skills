# Sustainability, Wellness, and IEQ

Prioritize durable, healthy, low-impact interiors over green theater.

## Levers
- Low-emitting materials and wet-applied products
- Daylight, glare control, and circadian-friendly lighting design intent
- Thermal comfort and operable control where feasible
- Biophilia and views
- Durability, repairability, reuse, and local sourcing
- Acoustics as a wellness factor

## Method
1. Define wellness/sustainability goals with the client
2. Target the highest-impact rooms/surfaces first
3. Prefer reuse and longevity before novelty products
4. Separate marketing claims from documented performance

## Deliverables
Goal statement, material/IEQ recommendations, tradeoffs, documentation to request from manufacturers
