# Residential Interior Design

Design for how people actually live at home.

## Room lenses
- Living: conversation clusters, media glare, flexible hosting
- Kitchen: work triangle/zones, storage, lighting, durability
- Bedroom: sleep quality, storage, soft lighting, privacy
- Bath: wet zoning, storage, ventilation, aging-in-place hooks
- Kids/flex: change-over time, durable finishes

## Method
Lifestyle interview → plan/clearances → palette/lighting → FF&E → phasing for budget.

## Deliverables
Concept, layout notes, palette, lighting, shopping/phasing list, site verify list
