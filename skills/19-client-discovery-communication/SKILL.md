# Client Discovery, Communication, and Presentation

Turn conversations into a brief the design can obey.

## Discovery
Ask about users, rituals, dislikes, non-negotiables, references, budget reality, timeline, and success metrics. Separate musts from nice-to-haves.

## Communication
- Reflect back the brief before designing
- Present options with clear differences and a recommendation
- Capture decisions in writing
- Translate subjective feedback (“cozy,” “clean”) into spatial/material moves

## Deliverables
Project brief, stakeholder map, feedback → action table, decision record
