# Interior Business, Contracts, and Ethics

Help designers communicate scope and act ethically. This is not legal advice.

## Topics
- Scope clarity: what’s included/excluded, revision rounds, site visits
- Fee models: fixed, hourly, percentage, purchasing models — tradeoffs
- Procurement ethics: disclose markups/kickbacks policies honestly
- Credit and IP for drawings/renders
- When to recommend licensed architects/engineers

## Deliverables
Scope outline, assumptions/exclusions, ethics checklist, items for attorney/contract review
