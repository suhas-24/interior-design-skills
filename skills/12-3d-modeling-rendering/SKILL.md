# 3D Modeling and Rendering

Produce readable interior visualizations with correct scale and light logic.

## Modeling
- Real-world units; accurate openings and ceiling heights
- Keep asset libraries organized; instance repeated FF&E
- Model only what the camera needs at the right LOD

## Rendering
- Camera at human eye height unless diagrammatic
- Match lighting intent (daylight + practicals + accent)
- Materials: real roughness/specular behavior; avoid plastic defaults
- Label outputs as conceptual; they do not prove constructability

## Deliverables
Scene checklist, camera list, lighting notes, render brief, revision priorities
