# Construction Documents and Specifications

Organize documents so builders can price and build without guessing.

## CD mindset
- Drawings show where/how much; specs define quality/products/execution
- Schedules (finish, door, FF&E) must match tags on drawings
- Demo, new work, and existing clearly distinguished
- Details resolve junctions that plans cannot

## Completeness checks
Conflicts between plan/RCP/elevation; missing dimensions; unspecified wet-area transitions; hardware; blocking; accessibility clearances called out for verification.

## Boundaries
Do not stamp or claim AHJ approval. Flag legal/engineering items for licensed professionals.
