# AI, Computer Vision, and Generative Interior Design

Use AI as a studio assistant, not an oracle.

## Good uses
- Rapid concept options and mood exploration
- Prompted redesigns that preserve architecture
- Organizing references and schedules
- Drafting layout alternatives for human critique

## Guardrails
- Measure and verify; models hallucinate dimensions and products
- Disclose AI-generated visuals to clients
- Respect IP/licenses of training data and assets
- Never treat CV room scans as construction-accurate without QA

## Deliverables
AI workflow plan, prompts, review checklist, human verification steps
