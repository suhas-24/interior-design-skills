# Materials, Finishes, and Textiles

Specify materials that earn their place: look, touch, durability, cleaning, and context.

## Selection criteria
Aesthetics, durability, maintenance, slip/fire/emission considerations (as applicable), moisture, UV, acoustics, cost, lead time, repairability, and sustainability.

## Method
1. Define performance requirements by room and surface
2. Build a coherent family (woods, metals, stones/soft goods)
3. Check junctions and transitions (floor changes, wet areas, edges)
4. Note care instructions and replacement strategy
5. Call out samples and mockups needed

## Deliverables
Material palette, room-by-room finish intent, maintenance notes, sample list, open spec questions
