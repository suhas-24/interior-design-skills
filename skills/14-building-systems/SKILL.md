# Interior Building Systems

Coordinate; do not engineer. Help interiors fit real systems.

## Coordination themes
- Ceiling clearance vs ductwork and lighting
- Supply/return locations vs furniture and acoustics
- Wet walls, stack logic, and fixture rough-ins
- Power/data densification for workplace and hospitality
- Sprinkler/head conflicts with aesthetic ceilings
- Access panels and maintenance clearances

## Deliverables
Coordination issue list, RCP integration notes, questions for MEP/FP engineers, owner decision items
