# CAD, BIM, and Technical Drafting

Help produce clear, coordinated interior drawings. Prefer clarity over decorative drafting.

## Practices
- Consistent units, scale, north, title blocks, and naming
- Plans, RCPs, elevations, sections, details, schedules — each with a purpose
- Layer/workset discipline; separate existing vs new vs demo
- Dimension only what construction needs; avoid redundant dims
- For BIM/IFC: track levels, types, and clash-prone categories (ceilings, casework, MEP)

## Deliverables
Drawing index suggestion, standard checklist, redline themes, coordination questions for consultants
