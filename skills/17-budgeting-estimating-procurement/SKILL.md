# Budgeting, Estimating, and Procurement

Make money decisions explicit. Separate estimate classes from bids.

## Practice
- Organize by CSI/division or room + FF&E vs construction
- Use allowances where selections are open
- Show base / target / stretch tiers
- Track lead times, freight, tax, install, contingency
- Value-engineer by priority, not by random cuts

## Deliverables
Budget table, assumptions, VE options with design impact, procurement timeline, risks (fx, freight, custom lead times)
