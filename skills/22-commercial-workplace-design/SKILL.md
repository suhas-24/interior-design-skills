# Commercial Workplace Design

Balance focus work, collaboration, culture, and change management.

## Program ingredients
Workpoints, meeting rooms, phone booths, lounge, amenity, support, storage, and brand moments. Right-size for hybrid utilization assumptions (label them).

## Design drivers
- Acoustic separation for focus
- Power/data density and cable paths
- Inclusive circulation and wayfinding
- Furniture standards vs variety
- Resilience for restack

## Deliverables
Program ratios, zoning, typical workstation/room types, finish/brand notes, coordination questions
