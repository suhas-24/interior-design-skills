# Hospitality Interior Design

Design the guest journey and the service journey together.

## Priorities
- Arrival → dwell → depart story
- Durable finishes at high-touch zones
- Lighting for daypart moods
- Acoustics for energy vs intimacy
- Back-of-house adjacency and staff efficiency
- Brand signature without maintenance nightmares

## Deliverables
Concept narrative, guest-flow notes, material durability map, lighting moods, FF&E direction
