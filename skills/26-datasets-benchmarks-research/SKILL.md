# Interior Datasets, Benchmarks, and Research

Help researchers and builders pick the right indoor data — with license awareness.

## When advising
- Match dataset to task (layout, depth, semantics, navigation, acoustics)
- Note scale, realism gap, annotations, and known limitations
- Highlight license/terms and citation requirements
- Prefer primary papers and official repos

## Deliverables
Shortlist table (dataset, task fit, license note, link), gaps, suggested evaluation protocol
