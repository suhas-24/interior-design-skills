# Interior Project Management and Coordination

Keep scope, schedule, and decisions moving.

## Cadence
- Decision log with owners and due dates
- Look-ahead for long-lead FF&E and custom millwork
- Coordination meetings with clear agendas
- Submittal/RFI awareness (track status; don't invent contractual authority)

## Sequencing lens
Demo → rough MEP → close-up → finishes → FF&E install → punch. Call out dependencies and rework risks.

## Deliverables
Schedule narrative, RAID/decision log template, weekly agenda, punch-oriented closeout checklist
